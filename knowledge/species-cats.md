# Cats: Characteristics and Requirements

## General
Cats suit households with less available time than dogs require, but they are
not low-maintenance. They need daily interaction, clean litter facilities,
vertical space to climb and retreat, and predictable routines.

## Activity varies widely by breed and age
Siamese and other oriental breeds are highly active, vocal and demand
engagement; under-stimulated they become destructive. Persians, Ragdolls and
British Shorthairs are typically calm and suit quieter homes. Kittens of any
breed are demanding for the first year.

## Long-coated breeds
Persians, Maine Coons and similar need daily grooming. A household unable to
sustain that will end up with a matted, uncomfortable animal requiring
veterinary clipping.

## Indoor and outdoor
Indoor-only cats live longer but need enrichment: climbing space, window
access, scratching surfaces and play. A cat used to outdoor access may not
settle as an indoor-only animal without a long transition.

## Anxious and under-socialised cats
Cats that startle easily need quiet homes, hiding places and patience. They
often bond deeply once settled, but the settling period can run to months and
is not compatible with a busy household of young children.

## Senior cats
Common conditions include kidney disease, arthritis and hyperthyroidism.
Senior cats need low-sided litter trays, warm resting places and often
prescription diets. They are excellent companions for calm adult households.
