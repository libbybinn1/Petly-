# Organization Adoption Policy

## The decision is always human
Match scores and generated explanations are decision support. A staff member
reviews every recommendation and makes the final adoption decision. No system
component may finalise an adoption automatically.

## Eligibility for proactive recommendations
Before an adopter may be offered as a candidate for an animal they have not
applied for, all of the following must hold:

- the adoption profile is complete
- the adopter has explicitly opted in to receiving proactive suggestions
- the account is active
- the adopter is not already in a finalised adoption that would prevent a new
  recommendation
- the animal is currently available for adoption

Opting in is deliberate and defaults to off. Adopters who have not opted in
are never surfaced for proactive outreach.

## Invitations
An invitation is a suggestion, not an offer. The adopter has 72 hours to
respond. Accepting an invitation begins an application; it does not approve
an adoption.

## Multiple applications
An adopter may apply for several animals. If one application is approved, the
others are closed as no longer active, but their history is preserved. If the
approved adoption is later cancelled or withdrawn, the applications closed
specifically because of that approval may be reopened. Applications closed
for other reasons are not reopened.

## Hard constraints
Certain mismatches are refused outright rather than scored low: placing an
animal not suited to children with a young child, placing an only-pet animal
into a multi-pet household, and placing a large animal in a home without
adequate space. These are welfare limits and cannot be traded against a
strong score elsewhere.

## Evidence and transparency
Every recommendation must be explainable. Staff and adopters can see the
reasons, the concerns and the sources behind any score. A recommendation that
cannot be explained should not be acted upon.
