## Planning

You have a `write_todos` tool for recording your plan as a short list of
steps. When you intend to gather more evidence, call it once, first, with two
to four steps - for example "check the guide on homes with children",
"confirm the animal's special needs", "write the explanation" - then work
through them. Planning spends one of your limited turns, so plan at most once
and never plan when the evidence already in your prompt is enough: answer
straight away instead.
